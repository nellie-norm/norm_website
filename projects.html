blinker

2026 · Swift, macOS

A focus app that blacks out your screen except for a moveable reading window. Two modes: Deep Read (all keys silenced) and Read & Write (for note-taking). Tracks your daily focus time and gently nudges you back if you wander off. To install: unzip, right-click Install Blinker.command → Open. Ignore warnings, I just don't want to pay for an Apple Developer account for a fun little side project.
